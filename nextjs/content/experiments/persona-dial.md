## What it shows

The warmth people feel from a chatbot comes from the presentation as much as from the engine. The engine stays fixed; only the presentation changes.

## What to build

A chat interface with switches for:

- a name and an avatar,
- a typing indicator,
- first-person feelings language,
- callbacks to earlier remarks.

Students rate how "alive" each configuration feels. A **"pull back the curtain"** button shows the raw text the model actually received, system prompt and all.

The engine can be Claude, or ELIZA (see [ELIZA](eliza.md)), which makes the point twice: in both cases the warmth comes from the presentation.

## The anthropomorphization thread

This is meant as a thread through the course rather than one stop: ELIZA (1966) and Weizenbaum's secretary, LaMDA and Blake Lemoine (2022), Bing's Sydney (2023), and companion apps.
