# Martin Puchner

**Harvard professor of drama, English, and comparative literature who teaches ENG 298AI; a historian of writing technologies and of drama, which shapes how the course reads chatbots.**

## Why it matters here

The course's angle comes from his work. *The Written World* (2017) is a history of literature as a series of writing technologies, which makes generative AI one more episode in a story that includes Theuth's letters; and a scholar of drama is well placed to read a chatbot as a performance, a character played in a dialogue. The syllabus pairs cultural history (Shelley, Wilde, Čapek), the history of dialogue (Plato, Confucius), and linguistics (Grice; Sacks, Schegloff, and Jefferson) with hands-on labs, and asks students to document and reflect on every use of AI.
