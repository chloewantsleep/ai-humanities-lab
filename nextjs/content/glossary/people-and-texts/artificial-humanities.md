# Artificial Humanities (Beguš)

**Nina Beguš's 2025 book reading fiction about artificial beings alongside actual language technology, from Eliza Doolittle to ELIZA to today's language models.**

## Why it matters here

The syllabus names it as further reading for the first section, "AI in Cultural History," and it is close to the course's own method: read the old stories about made beings as serious thinking about language and AI, and test them against the systems that now exist. The line it draws is a useful one for these experiments: Weizenbaum named ELIZA after Shaw's Eliza Doolittle, the flower seller taught to pass by speaking correctly, so the first chatbot was named for a story about passing through language.

## In the readings

Further reading for Seminar 4 (September 29); not in the course repository, so no passage is cited here. The book is open access from the publisher.
