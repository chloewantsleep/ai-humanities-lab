# The Policeman's Beard Is Half Constructed (1984)

**A 1984 book of prose and poems credited to Racter, a program by William Chamberlain and Thomas Etter: the first book marketed as written by a computer, and an early case of hidden human editing.**

## Why it matters here

It belongs in a course about authorship and AI for the questions it raises rather than its prose. The book's author line names a program; its introduction is by Chamberlain; and later scholarship argues that the published text was selected and shaped by people from much larger output, probably through templates Chamberlain wrote, and that the program sold to the public could not have produced it. "Who wrote this?" turns out to need a description of a process (generation, selection, editing, framing), which is the same description a student owes when they submit work made with an AI.
