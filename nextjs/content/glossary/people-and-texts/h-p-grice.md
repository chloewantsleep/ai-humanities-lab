# H. P. Grice

**British philosopher of language (1913–1988), later at Berkeley, who explained how people mean more than they say: the Cooperative Principle, the maxims, and implicature.**

## Why it matters here

Grice is the course's guide to the listener's side of a chatbot conversation. His account says that understanding depends on assuming the other party is cooperating and has a point, and that we will work hard to find one. That assumption is what a chatbot borrows: people supply the relevance, the intention, and sometimes the mind. His maxims also turn up, renamed, in the goals set for assistants (be informative, be truthful, be relevant, be clear).

There is a Harvard link too: "Logic and Conversation" comes from his William James Lectures at Harvard in 1967. The lectures were collected in *Studies in the Way of Words* (Harvard University Press, 1989).
