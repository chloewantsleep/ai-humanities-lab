# Streaming

**Delivering a response in pieces as it becomes available.**

## In plain terms

Rather than waiting for a complete response, an application can receive incremental events and display text as it arrives. A stream can carry tool-related events as well as prose.

Imagine the Course TF Assistant beginning its explanation while the final paragraph is still pending. Watch the delivery separately from whether the explanation is correct.
