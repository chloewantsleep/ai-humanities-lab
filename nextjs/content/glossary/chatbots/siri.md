# Siri

**Apple's voice assistant, introduced with the iPhone 4S in October 2011 as "an intelligent assistant that helps you get things done just by asking"; it began as a standalone iPhone app from a spin-off of SRI in February 2010.**

## In plain terms

Siri first appeared on 4 February 2010 as a free iPhone app from Siri Inc., a company spun off from SRI, where the underlying technology had been developed with DARPA grants. You spoke a request, such as finding a restaurant and booking a table or ordering a taxi, the app turned the speech into text, and it passed the request to some 30 web services. Apple bought the company that April.

Apple announced the iPhone 4S on 4 October 2011 with Siri built in, labelled a beta because it took only English, French, and German. Its examples were small chores: setting a reminder to call someone when you leave the office, reading out messages, making a calendar appointment. The assistant had a name and a voice from the start.

Large language models came later. With iOS 18 in 2024 Apple added a hand-off to ChatGPT (verify the date); in January 2026 Apple and Google said Gemini models would underpin future Apple foundation models, "to power AI features like Siri"; and on 8 June 2026 Apple announced "Siri AI," "an entirely new version of Siri," for developer testing, with a public beta promised later in the year.

## Where it fits

**Voice and public presence.** Siri put a named assistant you talk to into the iPhone, years before chatbots could hold a conversation. It made "ask the assistant" an everyday gesture, and its 2026 rebuild on a language model shows the older voice assistants being refilled with the newer engines.
