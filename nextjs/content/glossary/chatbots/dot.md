# Dot (New Computer)

**A personal AI companion app for iPhone from the startup New Computer (Sam Whitmore and Jason Yuan), built around long-term memory of its user; on waitlist in 2023, released in 2024, shut down 5 October 2025.**

## In plain terms

Dot was a chat app whose selling point was that it remembered. New Computer's 2023 site described "an intelligent guide designed to help you remember, organize, and navigate your life," and its founders' letter promised "a living history." The app ran on cloud models (the letter named OpenAI, Anthropic, and open-source options) and was on a waitlist through late 2023; by July 2024 the site said "Available now. Dot for iOS."

The founders' own example shows what memory meant in practice. Sam's Dot suggested she skip a run; she told it that her streak was over 500 days and "that's like the #1 thing you should know about me," and asked it to say "you will feel much better after you run!" next time. Dot replied, "I'll make sure to remember that."

In 2025 the founders announced that their visions had diverged and that Dot "will remain operational until October 5," inviting users to download their data. The notice called Dot a "friend, confidante, and companion."

## Where it fits

**Memory and companionship.** Dot made remembering the user the whole product rather than one feature among many, and its shutdown showed what is lost when a companion with your history is switched off. It is unrelated to OpenAI's [dots](openai-dots.md) (2026), which are agents rather than companions.
