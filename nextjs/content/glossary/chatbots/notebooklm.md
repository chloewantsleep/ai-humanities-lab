# NotebookLM (Gemini Notebook)

**Google's research notebook, previewed as Project Tailwind at I/O 2023 and released as NotebookLM that July: a chatbot that answers from the sources you upload, with citations. Renamed Gemini Notebook in July 2026.**

![The Gemini Notebook home page at notebook.google.com, October 2026, with featured and recent notebooks](../../history/_images/40-gemini-notebook-formerly-notebooklm-2026.png)

## In plain terms

You make a notebook, add sources (documents, notes, later YouTube links and audio files), and chat with a model that is meant to answer from those sources and point back to them. Google's launch post called this "source-grounding" and said it "effectively creates a personalized AI that's versed in the information relevant to you." The first release, on 12 July 2023, went to "a small group of users in the U.S."

In September 2024 Google added Audio Overview: "With one click, two AI hosts start up a lively 'deep dive' discussion based on your sources." The podcast-style conversation between two synthetic voices became the product's best-known feature.

On 16 July 2026 Google renamed it Gemini Notebook, kept it as a standalone product, tied it to the Gemini app and Search, and gave each notebook "a secure cloud computer" that can write and run code over its sources. The screenshot shows the new name at notebook.google.com.

## Where it fits

**Configuration and voice.** NotebookLM is a chatbot defined by what you feed it rather than by a persona: the same idea as the knowledge files in custom GPTs, Claude Projects, and Gems, but with the sources at the center of the interface. Audio Overview then gave those sources a pair of voices, a persona added after the fact. The 2026 cloud computer moves it toward the agents at the end of the story.

## In the readings

In the *Phaedrus* (274e–275a), Thamus tells the inventor of writing that letters are "an aid not to memory, but to reminiscence," and that their users "will appear to be omniscient and will generally know nothing" ([Jowett translation](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/course-readings/course-readings-md/plato-phaedrus.md#L894)). A notebook that answers questions about your own sources is a direct test of that worry: a written store that can, after all, be put a question.
