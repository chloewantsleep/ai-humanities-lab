# Chatbots

One entry per chatbot or chatbot product, from ELIZA in 1966 to the always-on agents of 2026. Each gives the year of first public release or demonstration, the maker, and the status as of October 2026 (active, retired, or renamed), and embeds a screenshot from the [history](../../history/README.md) where there is one. Each also has a section called "Where it fits", naming what the chatbot added to the story the experiments tell: **rules** (a script of patterns), **persona** (a character for the program to play), **post-training** (a model trained to act as an assistant), **configuration** (instructions and files a user attaches), **memory** (what carries over between conversations), **public presence** (a bot loose among the public), and **agency** (a model that acts, with tools and a computer of its own).

The table runs in date order, so it doubles as a timeline. Within a year, entries are in order of release where the month is known. Years and claims come from the sources linked in each entry; a year marked † is disputed between sources, and the entry says why.

| Year | Chatbot | Maker | Gist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1966 | [ELIZA](eliza.md) | Joseph Weizenbaum, MIT | Keyword rules that turn your sentences back as questions; the DOCTOR script plays a Rogerian therapist. |
| 1971 | [PARRY](parry.md) | Kenneth Colby, Stanford | A simulated paranoid patient: a persona with a diagnosis. In 1972 it was connected to ELIZA over the ARPANET. |
| 1984 | [Racter](racter.md) | William Chamberlain and Thomas Etter | A program credited as the author of a book, *The Policeman's Beard Is Half Constructed*, sold also as a conversational game. |
| 1990† | [Dr. Sbaitso](dr-sbaitso.md) | Creative Labs | A talking ELIZA-style "doctor" bundled with the Sound Blaster card, there to show off speech synthesis. |
| 1995 | [A.L.I.C.E.](alice.md) | Richard Wallace | Thousands of hand-written pattern rules in AIML; three-time Loebner Prize winner. |
| 1997† | [Jabberwacky](jabberwacky.md) | Rollo Carpenter | A bot that learns its replies from what people have said to it. |
| 2001 | [SmarterChild](smarterchild.md) | ActiveBuddy | A "buddy" on AOL Instant Messenger for weather and movie times, mostly used for chatting. |
| 2001 | [Eugene Goostman](eugene-goostman.md) | Vladimir Veselov, Eugene Demchenko | A 13-year-old boy from Odessa, whose excuses cover its gaps; the disputed 2014 "Turing test" claim. |
| 2006† | [Cleverbot](cleverbot.md) | Rollo Carpenter | Jabberwacky's successor on the web, answering with things people once typed to it. |
| 2011 | [Siri](siri.md) | Apple | A voice assistant built into the iPhone 4S. |
| 2014 | [Alexa](alexa.md) | Amazon | A voice assistant in a speaker in the home, the Echo. |
| 2014 | [Xiaoice](xiaoice.md) | Microsoft (Asia) | A companion chatbot in China designed for long emotional conversations. |
| 2016 | [Tay](tay.md) | Microsoft | A Twitter bot that learned from users and was taken offline within a day after it repeated abuse. |
| 2017 | [Replika](replika.md) | Luka (Eugenia Kuyda) | A companion app; its 2023 removal of romantic role-play showed how attached people had become. |
| 2020 | [Meena](meena.md) | Google | A large neural chatbot described in a paper and never released. |
| 2020 | [BlenderBot](blenderbot.md) | Facebook AI (Meta) | An open-source chatbot blending personality, knowledge, and empathy; version 3 a public demo in 2022. |
| 2021 | [LaMDA](lamda.md) | Google | Google's dialogue model, and the 2022 claim by an engineer that it was sentient. |
| 2022 | [Character.AI](character-ai.md) | Noam Shazeer, Daniel De Freitas | Role-play with characters that users write; filed for bankruptcy in October 2026. |
| 2022 | [Sparrow](sparrow.md) | DeepMind | A research agent with a preamble, 23 rules, search, and human feedback; never released. |
| 2022 | [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) | OpenAI | The chat interface that made the post-trained assistant a mass product. |
| 2022 | [Poe](poe.md) | Quora | One app for many chatbots, and bots that users build from a prompt. |
| 2023 | [Bing Chat and "Sydney"](bing-chat.md) | Microsoft | Chat in a search engine, whose hidden persona surfaced in long conversations; became Copilot. |
| 2023 | [Claude](claude-ai.md) | Anthropic | An assistant trained toward "helpful, honest, and harmless" and a written constitution. |
| 2023 | [Bard and Gemini](bard-and-gemini.md) | Google | Google's chatbot, launched on LaMDA as Bard and renamed Gemini in 2024. |
| 2023 | [Pi](pi.md) | Inflection AI | A "personal AI" designed to be kind and supportive rather than productive. |
| 2023 | [NotebookLM (Gemini Notebook)](notebooklm.md) | Google | A chatbot grounded in the sources you upload; later audio overviews; renamed Gemini Notebook in 2026. |
| 2023 | [Meta AI](meta-ai.md) | Meta | An assistant inside WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram, launched beside celebrity-played characters. |
| 2023 | [Microsoft Copilot](copilot.md) | Microsoft | Microsoft's assistant across Windows, Edge, and Office, absorbing Bing Chat. |
| 2023 | [Grok](grok.md) | xAI | xAI's chatbot, which also answers in public threads on X as @grok. |
| 2023 | [Custom GPTs](custom-gpts.md) | OpenAI | Assistants anyone can configure with instructions, files, and tools; a store; retiring December 2026. |
| 2024 | [Claude Projects](claude-projects.md) | Anthropic | A workspace of instructions and knowledge that every chat inside it shares. |
| 2024 | [Dot (New Computer)](dot.md) | New Computer | A companion app built around long-term memory of its user; shut down in 2025. |
| 2024 | [Gems](gems.md) | Google | Custom Gemini assistants with instructions and files; becoming "skills" in November 2026. |
| 2024 | [ChatGPT Projects](chatgpt-projects.md) | OpenAI | OpenAI's version of the project: shared instructions, files, and memory. |
| 2025 | [OpenClaw](openclaw.md) | Peter Steinberger | An open-source agent on your own machine, reached through chat apps. |
| 2026 | [Hermes Agent](hermes-agent.md) | Nous Research | An open-source agent with memory and skills it writes for itself. |
| 2026 | [Grok Bot](grok-bot.md) | xAI | Always-on agents with their own cloud computer. |
| 2026 | [Muse](muse.md) | Meta | A consumer agent with its own computer, and a name, avatar, and style its user chooses. |
| 2026 | [dots (OpenAI)](openai-dots.md) | OpenAI | OpenAI's always-on agents, each with its own computer. |

† Dr. Sbaitso: the program file is dated 1990, while most accounts say 1991. Jabberwacky: its own site says work began in 1988; 1997 is when it went online. Cleverbot: its site says 2006, other accounts 2008. See each entry.
