# Alexa

**Amazon's voice assistant, announced on 6 November 2014 inside the Echo, a speaker that stays on and wakes when you say "Alexa"; rebuilt on large language models as Alexa+ from 2025.**

## In plain terms

The Echo was a voice-activated speaker that could be left on all day. Saying the wake word, "Alexa," started it listening; it played music, kept a shopping list, and answered simple questions. At launch it cost $199, or $99 for Prime members, and was sold by invitation only. Where Siri lived in a phone you took out, Alexa lived in the kitchen and was addressed by name across the room.

On 26 February 2025 Amazon announced Alexa+, "your personal AI assistant that gets things done," running on large language models from Amazon Bedrock and able to "orchestrate across tens of thousands of services and devices." It costs $19.99 a month and is free with Prime. By Amazon's July 2026 update it was available to all customers in the US and Canada, with early access in several European and Latin American countries.

## Where it fits

**Voice and public presence.** Alexa made a named assistant part of the household: always listening for its name, spoken to rather than typed at. Alexa+ is the same name and voice refilled with a language model and pointed at actions, the turn toward [agents](../interfaces-and-agents/agent.md).
