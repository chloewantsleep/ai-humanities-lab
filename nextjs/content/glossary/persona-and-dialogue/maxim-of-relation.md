# Maxim of relation

**The expectation that a contribution is relevant to the exchange.**

## In plain terms

A reply mentioning a nearby library can help someone find a book without literally answering whether the speaker owns it. Readers look for a connection. With ELIZA, ask how much of that connection is supplied by the reply and how much by your interpretation.

## In the readings

Grice, p. 46, names relevance while stressing its unresolved difficulties. His garage example on p. 51 shows a hearer connecting an apparently indirect contribution to a practical need. Relevance depends on the situation, not merely shared keywords.
