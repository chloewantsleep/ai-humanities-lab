# Reasoning models

Reasoning models can generate intermediate steps before their final response. OpenAI introduced o1 in September 2024 and reported improvements on several reasoning tasks. Its interface showed a summary of those steps. [Read the announcement](https://openai.com/index/learning-to-reason-with-llms/).

When you use a reasoning model, check the final answer against the task. A longer response or a displayed summary can still contain errors.
