# GPT-2 with and without the HHH prompt: recorded samples

`hhh-gpt2.json` holds six unedited runs of the timeline's `demo: hhh` panel (`app/story/HhhDemo.jsx`), recorded 20 Oct 2026 in headless Chromium (WASM) on Madeleine's Mac by driving the page itself: `Xenova/gpt2` (GPT-2 small, q8), temperature 0.7, top-k 40, 90 new tokens. Column 1 is the question plus a newline. Column 2 is the opening paragraph of the HHH prompt (`app/by-hand/hhh-prompt.txt`) and its last 6 of 57 exchanges (about 910 tokens, all that fits GPT-2's 1,024-token window with room for the answer), then the question as a `Human:` turn, stopped at the next `\nHuman:` or `\n-----`. Odd apostrophes (`I’t`) are GPT-2's own output: the prompt uses curly quotes. The panel shows these before the reader loads the model; a live run replaces them.
