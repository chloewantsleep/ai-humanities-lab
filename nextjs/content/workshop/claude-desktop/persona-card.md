# Persona card

Fill this in **before** you open Claude. The card is the design, and the project's instructions are your translation of it. Print it from [/print/workshop/claude-desktop/persona-card](/print/workshop/claude-desktop/persona-card).

**Your name:** ______________________ **Bot's name:** ______________________

## Who it is

In one sentence: who is this character, and where do they come from (a reading, a role, an invention)?

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

## Who it talks to

Who is the user, and why have they come to this bot? (A student before an exam, a visitor to Rossum's factory, someone who wants advice from Lord Henry...)

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

## What it knows: the knowledge

Which files will you upload? Which parts of them matter most? (See the [course texts](texts.md).)

| File | Why this one |
|---|---|
| | |
| | |

What should it do when the files **don't** have the answer: say so in character, say so plainly, or something else?

&nbsp;

## How it speaks: the voice

Register, sentence length, vocabulary, habits, a phrase it repeats, words it never uses. Is it a **flat** character (one trait, always the same) or a **round** one (it can surprise you)?

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

## What it won't do

Topics it refuses, things it won't pretend, lines it won't cross.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

## "Are you an AI?"

What does it answer if someone asks what it really is?

&nbsp;

## Three exchanges, written before you build

Write the user's line *and* the bot's reply yourself. After you build, ask the same three questions and compare.

| 3 (a hard one) | | |

## Now turn it into instructions

Use the shape on the [main page](/workshop/claude-desktop) (step 3). Every line of the instructions should come from a box on this card. If a box has nothing in it, the bot will fill that gap itself.

## After testing: what changed?

What did you have to add to the instructions, and why? What came from the files that you didn't expect?
