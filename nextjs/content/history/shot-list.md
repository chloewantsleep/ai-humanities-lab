# The shot list

The [original capture record](https://github.com/ll-26-27/mk-chatbot-experiments/blob/26656e38f6fc522e65602fb844c09c5c43f56896/nextjs/content/history/shot-list.md) reports capture/download on **19 Oct 2026**. That is the recorder’s date, not a product launch date; task J inspected the saved PNGs, not the authenticated sessions. Historical dates below have separate sources.

This is the corrected task J inventory. The [original, unedited inventory](https://github.com/ll-26-27/mk-chatbot-experiments/blob/26656e38f6fc522e65602fb844c09c5c43f56896/nextjs/content/history/shot-list.md) preserves the recorder’s delivery; this page is no longer described as verbatim. Inspection confirms **43 PNG files**: **39 at 2880×1716** and four downloaded assets with the smaller dimensions listed below. The original record reports Chrome at 1440×900 with the tab strip removed and URL bar retained; those CSS-window dimensions and the capture date cannot be independently recovered from the PNG pixels alone.

Launch dates describe products or publications, never the age of the pictured UI. “Observed” means visible in the saved asset. “(verify)” means unresolved, with the reason stated. Images have not been replaced, recropped, or renamed.
