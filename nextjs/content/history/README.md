Sixty years of talking to machines through a text box, in screenshots: ELIZA's script, the first weeks of ChatGPT, the "build your own" products (custom GPTs, Claude Projects, Gems), and the agent products announced in 2026. Each section below has its images with a corrected caption and a sourced date. Two dates worth knowing today: custom GPTs are scheduled to retire on **11 Dec 2026** ([OpenAI's FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001519-custom-gpt-retirement-and-migration-faq); approved Enterprise deferrals to 11 Feb 2027), and Gems move to Settings for Workspace accounts on **17 Nov 2026** ([Google's transition schedule](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/gemini-app/about-the-transition-to-skills)), which is a move, not a universal sunset.

## About these images

The screenshots were captured or downloaded on **19 Oct 2026** by an agent using accounts available in Marlon's Chrome; that provenance comes from its [original capture record](https://github.com/ll-26-27/mk-chatbot-experiments/blob/26656e38f6fc522e65602fb844c09c5c43f56896/nextjs/content/history/shot-list.md), not an independent audit. A later check (task J) inspected all 43 PNGs (39 at 2880×1716, four downloaded assets smaller) and corrected three captions: the supposed early ChatGPT login is a GitHub profile, the supposed launch-dialogue sample is another article-header view, and the supposed ELIZA code page is an archival cover sheet. The filenames are unchanged. Copilot and Character.AI are landing or sign-in views; dots and Muse are announcement pages. Vendor feature descriptions are the vendors' claims, not tests. Launch dates are product or publication dates, never the age of the pictured interface.

The [shot list](shot-list.md) has every image with its source and correction; the [fact-check ledger](fact-check.md) also covers the planning note's model and agent claims. The images are in `nextjs/content/history/_images/`; reference them from this folder as `_images/<file>.png`, or from elsewhere in `content/` as `../history/_images/<file>.png`.
